1. Parties & Relationship

This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between Groove Hub Media Co. ("Provider / Agency") and the commissioning Client ("Client"). By executing an order, approving an estimate, or remitting payment against an official invoice, the Client accepts and agrees to these terms in full.

2. Scope of Services & Milestones

Groove Hub delivers creative post-production, high-end video editing, visual effects, color grading, sound engineering, and promotional asset creation as designated in the applicable Statement of Work (SOW) or invoice. Production timelines commence upon confirmation of initial milestone deposit and receipt of all required project assets.

3. Payment Schedule & Escrow Terms

Engagements are structured on a 50% upfront retainer / deposit to schedule studio rendering pipelines, with the remaining 50% due upon preview approval prior to unwatermarked master file release. Invoices are payable within seven (7) business days. Overdue balances may incur a late surcharge of 1.5% per month.

4. Revisions & Approval Policy

Each milestone deliverable includes up to two (2) complimentary rounds of consolidated revisions submitted within five (5) days of preview dispatch. Material scope alterations outside the initial creative brief will be quoted separately. Deliverables without feedback after seven (7) business days are deemed accepted.

5. Intellectual Property & Commercial License

Upon 100% full receipt of invoiced compensation, Groove Hub transfers full, perpetual, worldwide commercial exploitation and publishing rights to the final rendered exports to the Client. Client warrants legal rights to all provided footage/audio. Groove Hub reserves customary portfolio showcase rights unless an NDA is executed.

6. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

Both parties agree to treat all unreleased media, scripts, project timelines, and commercial figures shared during the project as strictly confidential. Neither party shall disclose such proprietary material without prior consent.

7. Limitation of Liability & Indemnification

The total aggregate liability of Groove Hub for any claim arising under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees actually collected for the specific project deliverable. Client indemnifies Groove Hub against third-party claims arising from client-provided materials.
