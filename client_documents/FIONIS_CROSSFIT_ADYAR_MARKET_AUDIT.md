## 3. What Attracts Walk-In Clients (Overcoming the Intimidation Barrier)

### The Hesitation Barrier:
OMR corridor IT professionals and techies (software engineers, product managers, QA leads from companies like TCS, Infosys, Zoho, Cognizant in Sholinganallur and Siruseri) often hesitate to try CrossFit because online posts show 100kg snatches and extreme gymnastic movements. Their immediate thought:
> *"CrossFit is too intense for me. I sit at a desk all day — I might injure my back or shoulder."*

### The Solution:
* **Coach Form-Correction Cues:** Short video reels showing the coach actively correcting hip hinges, squats, and shoulder alignment. Proves the gym is safe and coach-led.
* **Community Batch Energy:** Showcasing regular 30-to-45-year-old IT professionals in the 8:00 AM batch having fun and scaling exercises. Removes intimidation completely.
* **Google 3-Pack Review Boost:** Implementing an automated WhatsApp post-workout review request to add 10–15 five-star reviews every month so Fionis moves to the top of Google Maps OMR.
